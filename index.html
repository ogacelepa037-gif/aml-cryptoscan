FAQ

TRON AML Compliance Questions

Everything about TRON AML verification, TRC20 risk scoring, sanctions screening, and how AmlCryptoScan's platform works.

What is AmlCryptoScan?

AmlCryptoScan is a TRON network AML compliance tool. It checks a TRC20 wallet, USDT TRC20 address, or TRON transaction for AML risk, sanctions exposure, mixer usage, and other on-chain indicators, and returns a report. The first check is free; every check after that is $1.

How does TRC20 AML risk scoring work?

The engine analyzes the on-chain transaction history of a TRON address, traces fund flows through the TRC20 blockchain graph, checks against sanctions and watchlist sources, looks for mixer exposure and other risk patterns, and computes a composite AML risk score from 0 (clean) to 100 (high risk).

Does AmlCryptoScan support USDT TRC20?

Yes. AmlCryptoScan supports USDT TRC20 (Tether on the TRON network), TRX (native TRON), and other TRC20 tokens. You can check USDT TRC20 wallet addresses, transaction hashes, and contract interactions.

What TRON tokens can I check with AmlCryptoScan?

AmlCryptoScan supports TRC20 tokens including USDT TRC20 and other tokens deployed on the TRON network, as well as native TRX transactions. Token flows are analyzed together as part of the same wallet's transaction graph.

How much does a TRON AML check cost?

Your first TRON AML check is free, no card required. Every check after that is a flat $1, pay-as-you-go — no subscription. API access and bulk screening are priced separately; contact us for details.

What sanctions and watchlist sources does AmlCryptoScan screen against?

AmlCryptoScan screens TRON addresses against sanctions and watchlist data sources as part of every scan, including major government sanctions lists. Exact source coverage is documented in your account dashboard and API reference.

Is the first TRON AML check really free?

Yes. New accounts get one free TRC20 AML check with a full report, no credit card required. After that, each check is a flat $1, billed per use.

How fast is a TRON AML scan?

Most TRC20 wallet scans return a result within a few seconds. Wallets with very large transaction histories may take longer to complete a full graph trace. The PDF report is generated as part of the same scan.

What is included in the TRON AML PDF report?

Each TRON AML report includes: the wallet address and network (TRON/TRC20), an AML risk score (0–100), a risk category breakdown, sanctions check result, mixer exposure result, a summary of transaction flow, and a report timestamp.

Can I use the TRON AML check API for automated screening?

Yes. AmlCryptoScan provides a REST API for single TRC20 address checks and batch requests, with results available in JSON. Reach out for API documentation and access.

Who should use AmlCryptoScan?

Anyone working with TRON or USDT TRC20 who wants to check counterparty risk — including TRON-based exchanges, USDT TRC20 payment processors, OTC desks, P2P traders, fintech platforms accepting TRC20 payments, and individuals verifying a wallet before a large transfer.

Can AmlCryptoScan detect TRON mixer usage?

The scan looks for on-chain patterns consistent with known TRON mixing or obfuscation techniques, and traces downstream exposure where flagged funds appear in the analyzed transaction graph, not only in direct transactions.

Is AmlCryptoScan aligned with FATF virtual asset guidance?

AmlCryptoScan's report structure is designed with FATF-style virtual asset risk categories in mind (sanctions exposure, mixer usage, high-risk counterparties) to help support customer due diligence workflows. It is not a substitute for your own compliance program or legal advice.

What is the difference between direct and indirect TRON sanctions exposure?

Direct exposure means the checked wallet address itself appears on a sanctions list. Indirect exposure means the wallet has transacted with a sanctioned entity somewhere in the analyzed transaction graph. Reports indicate which type of exposure, if any, was found.

Can I check TRON DeFi wallets and smart contracts?

Yes. You can scan externally owned TRON wallet addresses, including ones that interact with TRON DeFi protocols. For contract addresses, the report focuses on counterparty risk based on wallets that funded or interacted with the contract.

How does TRON address monitoring work?

Monitoring subscribes a TRC20 wallet for ongoing review of new activity. If the monitored wallet sends to or receives from a flagged address — sanctioned, mixer-linked, or otherwise high-risk — you receive an alert via email or webhook.

Are AmlCryptoScan reports accepted by regulators?

AmlCryptoScan reports are structured to include the information commonly requested in due diligence records — address, network, risk score with category breakdown, sanctions status, and a timestamp. Requirements vary by jurisdiction and regulator, so we recommend confirming with your compliance advisor.

Can I white-label AmlCryptoScan reports?

White-labeling is available on request for teams that need to present TRC20 due diligence documentation under their own branding. Contact us to discuss enterprise options.

What happens if my TRON AML check returns a high-risk score?

A high-risk result breaks down the specific factors found — sanctions exposure, mixer usage, or dark-market links, for example. From there you can decide how to proceed, such as requesting additional due diligence from the counterparty or declining the transaction, based on your own compliance policy.

Does AmlCryptoScan protect user privacy?

AmlCryptoScan analyzes publicly available on-chain TRON data. No private keys are required or accepted. Scan results are tied to your account and are not shared publicly.

What is a TRON address AML risk score?

A TRON address AML risk score is a number from 0 to 100 summarizing the money-laundering risk indicators found for a specific TRC20 wallet or USDT TRC20 address, based on sanctions checks, mixer patterns, and counterparty exposure in the analyzed transaction graph.

Can I check if a TRON address is on a sanctions list?

Yes. Every AmlCryptoScan scan includes a sanctions check for the target TRON address and for counterparties found in its transaction graph, using the sanctions and watchlist sources the platform screens against.

How does TRON AML compliance differ from Ethereum AML?

Both networks require similar core checks — sanctions screening, mixer detection, counterparty risk — but TRON has its own risk patterns: heavy USDT TRC20 volume, low transaction fees that make rapid layering cheaper, and an account-activation model that differs from Ethereum's gas-based accounts.

What is USDT TRC20 AML compliance?

USDT TRC20 AML compliance refers to anti-money-laundering checks on Tether (USDT) transactions specifically on the TRON blockchain. Because USDT TRC20 is widely used for transfers, it's a common target for AML screening. AmlCryptoScan's checks map USDT TRC20 fund flows through TRON to look for sanctions exposure and mixer usage.

Does AmlCryptoScan index the full TRON blockchain history?

The platform's transaction graph is built from on-chain TRON data going back through the wallet's history, which supports tracing even for older wallets. Very large or very old wallets may take longer to process.

What is the TRON Travel Rule and does AmlCryptoScan support it?

The Travel Rule refers to FATF requirements for VASP-to-VASP transfers above a set threshold, requiring originator and beneficiary information. AmlCryptoScan's enterprise plans can support Travel Rule–related workflows for TRON VASPs handling USDT TRC20 and other TRC20 transfers; contact us for details.

Can AmlCryptoScan identify TRON ransomware-linked wallets?

The platform checks scanned addresses against known ransomware-linked wallet data where available. If a match or connection is found within the analyzed transaction graph, it's flagged as a high-risk indicator with the relevant fund-flow detail shown in the report.

Does AmlCryptoScan consider TRON account activation patterns?

TRON accounts require an activation transaction before they can send funds. Rapid, mass activation of new TRC20 wallets is one of the patterns the risk engine can factor into an address's overall assessment as a possible structuring indicator.

What integrations does AmlCryptoScan offer?

AmlCryptoScan offers a REST API with JSON responses, downloadable PDF reports, and webhook alerts for monitored wallets. Custom integration support is available for enterprise accounts — contact us to discuss your setup.

Is there a limit to how many TRON addresses I can check?

No hard limit on the pay-as-you-go plan — each check after your free first one is $1. For higher volumes, bulk and API plans offer better per-check economics; contact us for a quote based on your expected volume.

How do I get started with AmlCryptoScan?

Enter a TRC20 wallet address, USDT TRC20 address, or transaction hash into the scan widget above and run your free first check. No account or card is required for the first scan.
